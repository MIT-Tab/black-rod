# APDA OTY Scoring Guide

## Tournament points (base value)

Every varsity tournament has a "point" value tied to the varsity field size. The value equals the TOTY points earned by the champion team of two and the SOTY points earned by the top speaker. Events must feature at least eight teams to be worth points.

- 8–15 teams: 8 points
- 16–80 teams: `12 + floor((teams - 16) / 8)` points
- 80+ teams: capped at 20 points

All other placements and speaker awards scale off that base value.

## Team of the Year (TOTY)

- Champion = 100% of the base
- Finalist = base − 4
- Semifinalist = base − 9 (implemented as `3 + 0.75 * floor((teams - 16) / 8)` from 16–71 teams)
- Quarterfinalist = one quarter of the base

At the largest events the explicit tables are 19/15/8.25/3.5/0.75 (72–79 teams) and 20/16/9/4/1.5 (80+ teams). Only the five best tournament markers count toward a partnership’s season total. Hybrid teams (mixed schools) can win tournaments, but their points only flow into SOTY/COTY—not TOTY.

## Speaker of the Year (SOTY)

Speakers use the same base value. First speaker receives the base and every subsequent speaker drops by 2.5 points: base, base − 2.5, base − 5, etc. We track each debater’s six best varsity speaker finishes.

## Club of the Year (COTY) / Qual points

COTY reuses the team-points table but credits individual debaters. Every qualifying partnership that clears feeds its marker into each debater’s personal total. A debater may contribute at most 60 points to their school, but every autoqual (placing at or above the bid line at select BP/expansion tournaments) tacks on a 6-point bonus. That yields a maximum 66-point annual contribution. School totals are the sum of those capped contributions, so “qual points” and “COTY points” are the same quantity, though “qual points” often refers to the uncapped value prior to the 6-point autoqual bonus.

## Events without season points

- Nationals and British Parliamentary weekends award autoqual bids but **no** season points.
- Gender Minority, BIPOC, and similar invitationals award COTY (qual) credit only.

## General notes

- Tournament size drives the base, so enlarging a varsity field increases the reward for deep runs. Hosts typically abstain from competing at their own tournaments, but that rarely changes yearlong outcomes.
- Hybrid teams still grant SOTY/COTY credit to their members even though the partnership itself cannot bank TOTY placements.
