25th, 50th (median), and 75th percentiles for every season with usable scores.

How these statistics are calculated
  • Speaks are preliminary tournament totals; combined speaks sum both teammates. Panel scores are averaged per speaker per round before summing.
  • Only complete cards with positive speaks in every scheduled preliminary round count. Missing scores, duplicate round numbers, and elimination scores are excluded. Totals are not adjusted for different tournament lengths.
  • Breaking teams are teams with a recorded placing or elimination appearance, grouped by varsity or novice division. A team can appear in both distributions.
  • Breaking seeds are the highest and lowest varsity breaking seeds. Full-field seeds are the highest and lowest seeds among all teams with preliminary appearances.
  • Seeds are ordered by preliminary wins, combined speaks, then lower combined ranks when all candidates have ranks. Tied seeds are all included. A seed sample is omitted if any candidate has incomplete scores or an unknown outcome. Full-field samples also require the recorded field size to match preliminary appearances, when a field size is available.
  • Speaker places use recorded varsity and novice speaker results, including tied places. Missing placements are not inferred.
  • Sample size counts team-tournament or speaker-tournament observations for that row. Percentiles use linear interpolation between sorted observations; a single observation gives the same value for all three percentiles. A dash means no usable observations.
